🎯 Management Action Guide

Higher is Better ↑
Growth is normally favourable. If actual is below target, focus on the gap and identify the largest contributing area.
Lower is Better ↓
Reduction is normally favourable. For NPA, complaints, costs or delays, review the movement against target and previous period.
Neutral ↔
Interpret the movement in context. Do not label growth or decline as favourable unless the KPI definition supports it.

🔎 Data Quality & Review Checks

Before relying on a management conclusion, check the uploaded data for completeness and consistency.

Dates / Periods
Check that all reporting periods are present and consistently formatted.
Numeric Values
Check amounts, percentages and ratios for blanks, text or unexpected units.
Targets
Ensure target and actual figures relate to the same period and unit.
Duplicates
Review repeated records before using totals or category comparisons.
Missing Data
A blank value should not automatically be treated as zero.
Units
Confirm ₹, ₹ lakh/crore, counts and percentages are not mixed.
Category Names
Check spelling and naming consistency so categories are not split incorrectly.
Management Conclusion
Use chart insight only when the underlying data supports the conclusion.

🧭 Executive Review Summary

Use this three-part structure when presenting branch or bank performance to senior management.

Strengths
Identify KPIs where actual performance is favourable against target or previous period, considering whether the KPI is Higher or Lower is Better.
Watch Areas
Identify adverse movement, target gaps, rising complaints, weakening recovery or deterioration in asset quality.
Priority Actions
Focus management attention on material gaps and adverse trends first, then assign responsibility and a review date.

This is a review framework, not an automatic conclusion. Actual strengths, watch areas and actions must be supported by the uploaded data.

📐 KPI Interpretation Rules

Standard management calculations used when the required fields are available in the uploaded data.

Achievement %
Actual ÷ Target × 100
Use only when Actual and Target refer to the same period and unit.
Variance
Actual − Target
A positive variance is not automatically favourable; KPI nature matters.
Growth / Degrowth
(Current − Previous) ÷ |Previous| × 100
Compare like-for-like periods wherever possible.
KPI Nature
Higher ↑ / Lower ↓ / Neutral ↔
Interpret movement according to the KPI definition, not merely the sign of change.

Where the uploaded data does not contain the required comparison fields, InstantDash should display the available figure rather than manufacture a comparison.

❓ Help & guide

1. How to upload a file
Drag a .xlsx, .xls or .csv file anywhere on the page, or use "Browse for a file." Nothing is uploaded to a server — all parsing happens in your browser tab.

2. How header detection works
InstantDash scans the first 10 rows and picks the row most likely to be a header — mostly text, with the rows beneath it consistently filled in. You can override this with the "Header is on row" dropdown once a file is loaded.

3. How columns are classified
Every column is typed as Number, Date, Text/Category, or Boolean, then given a role (below) based on how many unique values it has and its name.

4. What is a Measure?
A numeric column that isn't an ID — something you'd sum or average, like Sales, Quantity, or Balance.

5. What is a Dimension?
A text or boolean column with 15 or fewer unique values — good for grouping and filtering, like Region or Status.

6. What is High Cardinality?
A text column with 16–50 unique values, like Product Name. Usable in charts, but only as a "Top N + Others" view to stay readable.

7. How filters work
Every Dimension gets a multi-select filter; every Date column gets a from/to range. All KPIs, charts, and the data table update live as you change them.

8. How charts are selected
Based on what's in your profile and the KPI database: ₹/INR/Rs/currency KPIs → Bar; other generic KPIs → Donut. Date charts can be grouped by Day/Month/Quarter/Year. Histogram and Pie are also available manually.

9. How to create a pivot
Click "Pivot table," choose Rows, Columns (optional), Values, and an aggregation, then "Generate pivot." Totals are always recalculated from the underlying records — never by adding up averages, minimums, or maximums.

10. What "Ask Your Data — Local Analysis" can answer
It reads the same profile and aggregates already on your screen — always from the currently filtered data — and explains them in plain language: contributors to a change, rankings by category, totals and averages. It does not use an external AI model; it's a local, rule-based reader of your numbers, so it can't reason beyond your columns. It can identify the largest contributor to a change, not necessarily the reason behind it — correlation shown in your data is not the same as a confirmed cause. Note: if you use the microphone button, the audio is processed by your browser or device's built-in speech-to-text service (e.g. Apple's or Google's), which is not guaranteed to be fully on-device — typed questions stay entirely local.

11. Privacy
Your spreadsheet is processed locally in your browser. InstantDash does not upload your spreadsheet to a server. It's held in your browser's memory only, and closing the tab clears it. (The page itself — its code, fonts, and the spreadsheet-reading library — loads from the internet the first time you open it, the same as any webpage; it's your file's contents that never get sent anywhere. If voice input is used with "Ask Your Data," that audio is processed by your browser/device's built-in speech recognition service, which may not be entirely local — see note 10.)

12. Supported file formats
.xlsx, .xls, and .csv, including multi-sheet workbooks (use the sheet selector).

13. Large-file limitations
InstantDash stays responsive up to roughly 100,000 rows by aggregating before charting and paginating the data table. Files beyond ~200,000 rows will show a warning and may feel slower.

14. Troubleshooting
If a chart looks wrong, check the Data profile panel — you can manually override any column's detected type. If numbers look off, check currency/thousands formatting in your source file, or adjust decimals/number format in Settings.
